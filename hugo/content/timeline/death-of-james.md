  note: "Britannica gives James's death as 62 CE, in Jerusalem, at the hands of priestly authorities, reputedly by stoning according to Josephus or by being thrown from a Temple tower according to Hegesippus. Schmidt says James was likely executed in 62 CE, because Josephus places his trial in the interval after the Roman governor Festus had died and before his successor arrived; Jackson-McCabe says James died, reportedly at the hands of an interim Judean authority around 62 CE. Schmidt notes that this contradicts Christian tradition, which, he says, puts James's death around 69 CE, shortly before (and even as the cause of) the destruction of Jerusalem: Hegesippus records that the execution came immediately before Vespasian's siege of Jerusalem, and Eusebius says Clement's account agrees."
