  note: "Britannica says the precise origin of assigning December 25 as Jesus' birth date is unclear, and that the date was first identified as such by Sextus Julius Africanus in 221. Roll, in the New Catholic Encyclopedia, says the earliest mention of the Nativity on December 25 can be found in the Chronograph of Philocalus, a Roman almanac whose source material can be dated to 336; Africanus, writing prior to 221, placed the annunciation and the passion on March 25, which, Roll writes, would point to a December 25 birth date. McGowan likewise says the earliest mention of December 25 as Jesus' birthday comes from a mid-fourth-century Roman almanac."
