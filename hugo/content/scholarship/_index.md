title: "Scholarship"
intro: 'Scholarship Pages are this site''s own work, not the show''s, and the Hosts have not reviewed them. Each one starts from something said on an Episode and sets out what published scholarship says about that subject, as a list of questions with short answers. Every statement rests on a scholarly book, a journal article or a reference work that can be read online, and a Transcript is never a source. Where the sources are more cautious than the show, the page follows the sources. A page says "consensus" or "most scholars" only where a cited source says so.'
