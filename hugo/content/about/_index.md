**Metadata over Dogma** is an unofficial companion site for the [Data Over Dogma](https://www.youtube.com/@dataoverdogma) podcast, hosted by Dr. Dan McClellan and Dan Beecher.

## What we do

This site provides searchable, browseable access to the metadata surrounding each episode — transcripts, topic tags, scripture references, recurring segments, and guest appearances. Our goal is to make the scholarly content of the podcast easier to find, reference, and revisit.

## How it works

Episodes are transcribed using AI-assisted tools, then corrected and tagged through a combination of automated and manual processes. Every transcript is reviewed for accuracy, and topics are mapped to a controlled vocabulary to keep the archive consistent and searchable.

## Scholarship Pages {#scholarship-pages}

Scholarship Pages are this site's own work, not the show's, and the Hosts have not reviewed them. Each one starts from something said on an Episode and sets out what published scholarship says about that subject, as a list of questions with short answers. Every statement rests on a scholarly book, a journal article or a reference work that can be read online, and a Transcript is never a source. Where the sources are more cautious than the show, the page follows the sources. A page says "consensus" or "most scholars" only where a cited source says so.

Every citation is checked twice, independently, before a page is published: the source is opened at the address given, the quoted passage is found word for word at its page or timestamp, and the page's wording is held to no more than the source says. A citation that can't be confirmed is cut. These checks are done with AI tools, not by a scholar. Each page shows the date its sources were read.
