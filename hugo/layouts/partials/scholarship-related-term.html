{{- /* The one rule matching a Scholarship Page's `related` entry to a term page. Takes a dict: taxonomy "books", "tags" or "topics" (the `related` key) term one entry from that list Returns the term page, or nil when the site has none. Hugo keys a term page by its raw name, lowercased with whitespace as hyphens; punctuation such as apostrophes stays (taxonomyFolderSlug in src/utils/title-to-slug.ts). urlize strips apostrophes, so it misses "Aaron's Rod" (/topics/aaron's-rod, published at /topics/aarons-rod/). */ -}} {{- $key := .term | strings.TrimSpace | lower | replaceRE `\s+` "-" -}} {{- return site.GetPage (printf "/%s/%s" .taxonomy $key) -}}
