{{- /* The Timeline minimap (#167 §1–§3): the whole Timeline drawn to time, a fixed rail in the inline-start gutter on wide screens, and on narrow screens a sticky "You are in" bar whose Contents button opens the same minimap as a popover. On wide screens a pill fixed over the axis names the current era. Era bands are sized by their year span and each placed entry is a tick at its year, so Hugo lays it all out; nothing is measured in the page. Takes the eras from timeline-eras.html. "You are here" is CSS. One hidden list of entry links is the only scrollspy (scroll-target-group); it stays rendered while the popover is closed, so the bar tracks the era with no geometry. The generated :has() rules below set one flag, --is-current: 1, on whatever its :target-current link makes current: the caption label, the bar's era, the band, the tick and the entry's narrated setting (#170). timeline-minimap.css says what the flag shows. Only entries are scroll targets, so the highlight never alternates between eras and entries. timeline-minimap.ts (loaded from the list layout's footer scripts) mirrors the current tick into aria-current. */ -}} {{- $eras := . -}} {{- $labels := partial "timeline-labels.html" -}} {{- $placedLabel := $labels.placed -}} {{- $start := float (index $eras 0).from -}} {{- $years := sub (float (index $eras (sub (len $eras) 1)).to) $start -}} {{- /* A tick is a dot; --tick-pitch (dot plus gap) is one dot's place: 0.035 of the shortest strip (14px of about 400px, in a 360×640 popover) and less on the rail (18px of 787px at 1728×1000; measured), so 0.04 here. Ticks closer than that once drew as one, as Creation (4004 BCE) and Adam (4000 BCE) did, so close ticks form a column: each dot sits a pitch below the one before, or at its own year if that is lower still. Unrolled, a dot's place is the largest of (an earlier tick's year + a pitch per tick between them, itself included), written out per tick as --tick-stack, since CSS cannot read another element's place. Only the earlier ticks that could win on the shortest strip are written; on a taller strip a pitch is a smaller share, so none of the others could win there either. --tick-after (ticks below it) keeps the column on the strip; see timeline-minimap.css. */ -}} {{- $pitchShare := 0.04 -}} {{- /* One flat model: eras with their ids, entries with their era, strip position, column place and caption date. */ -}} {{- $bands := slice -}} {{- $ticks := slice -}} {{- range $eras -}} {{- $eraId := partial "timeline-era-id.html" . -}} {{- $bands = $bands | append (dict "id" $eraId "label" .label "years" (sub .to .from) "empty" (not .items)) -}} {{- range .items -}} {{- $p := .page.Params -}} {{- $date := "" -}} {{- if eq .by "tradition" -}} {{- $date = printf "%s (%s)" $p.tradition.display (lower $placedLabel) -}} {{- else -}} {{- $date = $p.critical.display -}} {{- end -}} {{- $at := div (sub (float .year) $start) $years -}} {{- $terms := slice -}} {{- range $i, $earlier := $ticks -}} {{- $steps := sub (len $ticks) $i -}} {{- if lt (sub $at $earlier.at) (mul $steps $pitchShare) -}} {{- $terms = $terms | append (printf "%v * 100%% + %d * var(--tick-pitch)" $earlier.at $steps) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- $terms = $terms | append (printf "%v * 100%% + 0 * var(--tick-pitch)" $at) -}} {{- /* A narrated setting with years: a span at its years and an arc from its middle to the tick's dot. */ -}} {{- $setting := dict -}} {{- with $p.setting -}} {{- if isset .
