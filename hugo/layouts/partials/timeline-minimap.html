{{- /* The Timeline minimap (#167 §1–§3): the whole Timeline drawn to time, a fixed rail in the inline-start gutter on wide screens, and on narrow screens a sticky "You are in" bar whose Contents button opens the same minimap as a popover. On wide screens a pill fixed over the axis names the current era. Era bands are sized by their year span and each placed entry is a tick at its year, so Hugo lays it all out; nothing is measured in the page. Takes the eras from timeline-eras.html. "You are here" is CSS. One hidden list of entry links is the only scrollspy (scroll-target-group); it stays rendered while the popover is closed, so the bar tracks the era with no geometry. The generated :has() rules below set one flag, --is-current: 1, on whatever its :target-current link makes current: the caption label, the bar's era, the band, the tick, the entry's narrated setting (#170) and the entry on the axis. timeline-minimap.css says what the flag shows. Ticks closer than that would overlap, as Creation (4004 BCE) and Adam (4000 BCE) did, so each takes the lowest slot (--tick-slot) none of them has: a row of dots running inward from the tick track, one pitch apart.
